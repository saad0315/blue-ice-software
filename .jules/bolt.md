## 2024-05-18 - [Optimizing driver-view stats by minimizing parallel queries]
**Learning:** I found that `getDriverStats` was issuing multiple parallel database `.count()` and `.aggregate()` calls on `db.order` to get statuses (total, completed, pending, cancelled, rescheduled) and payment breakdowns. These separate parallel queries can become a performance bottleneck by unnecessarily increasing database connection usage. They can be effectively replaced with single `groupBy` queries combined with deriving the final metrics in-memory, mirroring previous dashboard optimizations.
**Action:** Always look for opportunities to replace multiple `.count()` or `.aggregate()` parallel database calls with a single `.groupBy()` when the difference is only in the filtering criteria.
