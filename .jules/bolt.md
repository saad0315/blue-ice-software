## 2024-05-19 - [Consolidating Dashboard Metric Derivations]
**Learning:** In the comprehensive dashboard queries, numerous aggregate database queries (such as counting completed orders, total volume, and deriving total revenue) were being issued alongside queries that already retrieved grouped data (e.g., `groupBy` on order status and payment method) and list data (e.g., `findMany` on daily stats and products).
**Action:** When building or optimizing high-level dashboard metric APIs, look for opportunities to derive sub-metrics (like total revenue or counts) in-memory from pre-fetched comprehensive list or grouped data (`groupBy` or `findMany`), rather than issuing parallel redundant database queries. Always ensure that in-memory filtering and sorting correctly mimic the displaced database queries.
